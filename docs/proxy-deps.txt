serve uses state::load and state::dir per INTERFACES.md. Route.name supports host, *.domain and host/path prefix (path preserved); bare names alias name.localhost. TLS generates ca.pem/ca-key.pem in state dir and issues cached certificates by SNI; trust installation belongs to integrations. Custom PEM pair supported. Bodies stream with backpressure; HTTP/1 upgrades use bidirectional tunnels; HTTP/2 accepted via ALPN and cleartext auto detection. Bind accepts IPv4/IPv6 addresses; OS dual-stack policy applies to IPv6 wildcard sockets.
